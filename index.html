40/60 Grading & Risk Policy

Calculations follow the school's configured 100-mark assessment structure.

Green Zone · CA 30–40

On Track for Excellence. The student requires 40 marks or fewer out of 60 in the final examination to reach 70%.

Yellow Zone · CA 20–29

Deficit Push Zone. The student requires 41–50 marks out of 60 to reach 70% and can still target the 50% pass baseline.

Red Zone · CA 0–19

Critical Baseline Risk. The student requires more than 30 examination marks to reach the 50% pass baseline.
